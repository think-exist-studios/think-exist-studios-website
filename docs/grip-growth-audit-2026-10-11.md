## Read-only verification checklist
1. From the Think Exist Studios / Dark Forest Pump.fun profile, establish the canonical minted address and matching creator / deployment transaction. Record transaction signature, UTC time, token decimals and token program; validate separately on a Solana explorer.
2. Confirm the linked project page and official public social channels display the **same** mint. Record screenshots or auditable hashes of public identity links.
3. Capture a UTC-stamped data table with holders (token accounts, not people), top 10 ownership with pool/exchange exclusions, developer holdings, liquidity, one- and 24-hour buy/sell counts, unique trading wallets if demonstrably available, volume, market cap, and migration stage. Unknown data stays **unknown**.
4. Determine launch-page status and whether reward distribution is deployed, funded and independently claimable. Separate planned vs active features, risks and eligibility.
5. Compare with Quantum Inu ($QI): 2026-10-09 launch; early archived >$1M market-cap and thousands of holder accounts. Its PQC attestation and crypto-trend meme likely helped differentiate it, but no causal attribution is demonstrated. Later exchange listings should not be credited for pre-listing price increases without timestamps.
6. Review whether visible activity is genuine: linked-wallet clusters, repeated wash-like buys/sells, creator and insiders, concentration and slippage. Volume alone is not proof of adoption.

## Budget-aware organic launch review (additional spend: $0 initially; ceiling $20 until separately authorized)
- **Trust first:** Fix stale page labels, confirm mint and creator on official links, explain current versus planned features, publish risk and fee notices. Avoid marketing anything that hasn't been shipped.
- **Genuine audience:** Useful visual posts featuring Professor HODL, short authentic Q&A, opt-in community discussions, consistent links and updates; no undisclosed paid promoters, inorganic followers, artificial trades, or scripts impersonating members.
- **Weekly honest scorecard:** UTC date, organic unique community participants, repeat engagement, verified holder accounts, holder concentration, net liquidity, organic referrals and available market activity. Record sources and gaps, not price targets.
- **If organic engagement grows:** evaluate transparent rewards delivery, accessible FAQs, code and contract audit, community feedback. Treat exchange applications and marketing spending as separate proposals requiring evidence and explicit cost approval.
