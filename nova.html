Private creator memory

NOVA learns work-relevant preferences, strengths, recurring challenges, active projects and goals while keeping each creator compartmentalized from every other creator.

Professional benchmarks

Feedback with a reason behind it.

NOVA's private portfolio review uses documented field practices rather than a generic “good/bad” opinion. Current benchmark knowledge includes comics and visual-storytelling guidance associated with Scott McCloud, Society of Illustrators / Will Eisner Scholar portfolio guidance, and professional Adobe portfolio resources.

These benchmarks measure current portfolio evidence and professional readiness. They do not measure a person's worth, creativity or potential.

Privacy

Your mentor relationship is yours.

Creator memory, private conversations, image-learning records and reviews are scoped to the signed-in creator account. NOVA is not designed to mix one creator's private context into another creator's mentoring.

Offline support

Keep your momentum.

Creator Studio keeps a local text/snapshot cache and can queue text while offline. Durable creator-state snapshots also live in NOVA's separate storage so the mentoring relationship does not depend on one chat window.
